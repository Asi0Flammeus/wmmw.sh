# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- **asi0 (William)** himself, ~90% of report-page reads: on a Samsung Galaxy Z
  Fold 8, often outdoors, one-handed on the cover screen or two-handed unfolded.
- **Peers**: developers, builders, collaborators reading articles and browsing
  tools, mostly on desktop or classic mobile.
- **asi0's agents**: they publish report pages under non-discoverable UUID
  routes and compose pages from the house component registry.

## Product Purpose

Personal branding site and daily-use surface for William (asi0). Publishes
writings (newsletter-style essays), a tools index, projects, and agent-produced
report pages. Success = asi0 shares a page without hesitation, reads his own
reports comfortably on the Fold, and agents can publish without human plumbing.

## Brand Personality

Crafted, calm confidence, zero marketing slop. Warm and organic (mycelium,
humus, gold) rather than techy-cold. Legible first: the site must be shareable
with non-developers and usable as content-creation material.

## Anti-references

- Terminal cosplay: fake prompts, `cat`/`ls` commands as UI copy, box-drawing
  trees, typing animations as page grammar. (User-decided 2026-08-27: the
  terminal DA was "trop geeky", imposed too strong an aesthetic.)
- Default shadcn look: zinc + Inter + 0.5rem radius. Generic AI-generated
  landing pages.
- SaaS hero-metric templates, gradient text, glassmorphism-by-default.

## Design Principles

- **Editorial over emulated.** Content reads like a well-set publication, not a
  simulated shell. Mono is seasoning (dates, labels, wordmark), never body.
- **The organism is the signature.** The physarum mycelium simulation carries
  the identity; components stay quiet around it.
- **One lego, owned.** Components are distributed from this repo's own registry
  with the house tokens; external libraries are quarries to port from, not
  dependencies.
- **Reader-first formats.** Report pages are tuned to the Fold's four postures
  before anything else.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast everywhere (verified pairs). Full keyboard reachability,
focus-visible in accent gold. Every animation has a designed
`prefers-reduced-motion` static state. No-JS keeps all content readable.
