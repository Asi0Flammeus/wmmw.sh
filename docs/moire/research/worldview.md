## 3. Moiré as the visual form of interdependence

**The physics, accurate and non-mystical:**
- **Two layers, a third pattern.** Overlay two periodic gratings with a slight offset in pitch or angle and you get a large-scale beat pattern. The fringe period is about p/θ for a small rotation θ. The pattern belongs to the *relation* between the layers and exists in neither layer alone.
- **Small offset, large effect.** Moiré is used in metrology to make tiny displacements visible, which is amplification.
- **Emergent properties.** Twisted bilayer graphene at the about 1.1° "magic angle" forms a moiré superlattice with emergent correlated states and superconductivity (Cao et al., *Nature* 2018). Neither sheet has that property alone.
- **The textile origin is also two layers.** Moiré silk is made by pressing two layers of fabric together.
- **Mycorrhiza** literally means fungus + root: a partnership that is neither plant nor fungus.
- **He is a physicist by training.** The manifesto already says "j'ai passé des années à chercher les structures cachées derrière ce qui se voit." Moiré is exactly a hidden structure made visible by overlay.

**Mapping:**

| Moiré property | Principle | Client translation |
|---|---|---|
| Exists only in the overlap | Interdependence (P3), third way (P5) | Your context combined with my craft gives a tool neither of us had |
| Identical, aligned layers produce *nothing* | Complementarity, not sameness (P4) | Your difference is the raw material; no cloned templates |
| Both layers stay intact | Distinct, never separate (P6) | You keep ownership of code and data; I keep my craft; no lock-in |
| A small shift makes a big visible change | Leverage (DII: "démultipliés, tel un effet de levier") | Small precise interventions, outsized effect |
| The pattern changes as the viewer moves | Identity as pattern, not component (P7) | You see who I am through the work, across projects |

**Naming bonus:** William Moiré = **W.M.**, so the WMMW palindrome (WM | MW, "Ce qui sort du réseau y revient") survives the persona change intact.

**Five brand lines** (no dashes; ES included because the site is trilingual):

3. FR « Ton contexte, mon métier : un outil qui n'existait pas. » (vous form: « Votre contexte, mon métier : un outil qui n'existait pas encore. ») / EN "Your context, my craft: a tool that did not exist yet." / ES "Tu contexto, mi oficio: una herramienta que aún no existía."

## 5. Risk list (where it reads esoteric, preachy or unprofessional) and mitigations

1. **"Wealth" + Bitcoin + Plan B can read as a crypto guru or financial advice.** Define wealth as five dimensions on first use. Never pair it with returns, ROI or "financial freedom". Frame Plan B Academy as open education he maintains. His own floor (`g2a-centauric-financial-autonomy/README.md`): nothing that requires a regulated qualification, financial included.
2. **The gift ethos blurs with paid work.** "sans exiger de retour" sitting next to an intake form confuses clients. State the split plainly: open work (writings, tools, code, Plan B) is given; client work is scoped and paid; reciprocity (referrals, feedback) is invited, never required.
3. **Eastern-spirituality sources (Wilber, Ram Dass, Laozi, satori) are esoteric.** Keep them backstage. Express the same ideas through **physics (moiré, interference)** and **biology (mycorrhiza)**, which matches his real credential.
4. **Preachy tone.** "Je crois", "règle de vie", "La forêt n'a pas besoin d'un arbre de plus" work in a manifesto but lecture on service pages. Keep the worldview on one page linked once from home. Elsewhere, show each principle as a behavior: open code, honest scope, handover, reply time.
5. **Breadth reads as dispersion ("touche-à-tout").** Use principle 7: one network (William Moiré), four organs (Plan B Academy, alysis, custom tools, Ghosts), one line each. The worldview is the visible thread.
6. **Metaphor overload.** The site already runs a physarum "mycelium" simulation. Physarum is a slime mold, not a fungus, which a pedant may notice. Assign roles: **moiré = visual signature**, **mycelium = name of the wealth idea**. Never stack both in one sentence.
7. **The Ghosts teaser can read as eerie sci-fi**, especially next to non-duality. One factual sentence plus "soon". Avoid "conscious", "alive", "soul".
8. **"comme si ma vie en dépendait"** reads as overwork or desperation in a client context. Keep it in the manifesto only, or soften it.
9. **Canon drift looks unserious** (emotional vs spiritual, mycelic vs mycelial, dimensions vs forms). Fix the canon from section 2 before writing any copy.
10. **Professional trust signals already exist in his own rules. Surface them as client benefits:**
    - "Le silence n'est pas un accord" (`g2a-nondual-mind/README.md`): no silent assumptions on scope.
    - "Public en refus par défaut": privacy by default.
    - "Une cellule vide est un résultat ; une cellule inventée est un défaut" (`g2a-centauric-financial-autonomy/README.md`): he says when he does not know.
    - The centaur speed claim ("six mois ... environ deux semaines"): usable only with proof cases.
