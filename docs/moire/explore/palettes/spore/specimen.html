SPORE1 · Strophaire vert-de-gris

One mushroom holds the whole palette: the verdigris agaric (Stropharia aeruginosa) has a blue-green cap, purple-grey gills and a purple-brown spore print. The page is the gill, the text is the spore print, and the verdigris accent is the cap, the one part that catches the eye. Distinct parts, one organism: the brand idea in a single species.

SPORE2 · Rhodochrosite

Sliced rhodochrosite stalactites show concentric rose bands laid down by rhythmic precipitation: a natural ring grating, close kin to the hero field radiating from the accent of the é. The palette is one warm hue at every depth, blush paper, raspberry action, wine-dark night, so the warmth of the person carries the page while the physics hides in the rings.

SPORE3 · Lichen géographique

The map lichen (Rhizocarpon geographicum) tiles Alpine schist in yellow-green, each colony fenced by a black line of fungal threads, and geologists date moraines by measuring it. A fungus and an alga living as a third organism, read with a ruler: interdependence and precision on the same stone, in the mountains above Chambéry. The chartreuse only ever sits on slate, so in light mode the button is a stone carrying lichen, and in dark mode the whole page is the stone.

SPORE4 · Érythrite

Erythrite (cobalt bloom, crimson) and annabergite (nickel bloom, pale green) are the two ends of one continuous mineral series: swap cobalt for nickel atom by atom and the colour slides from one to the other. The paper is the nickel end, the action is the cobalt end, and every page lives in between. Distinct, never separate, written in chemistry.

SPORE5 · Améthyste dans la mousse

The amethyst deceiver (Laccaria amethystina) trades with beech and oak through its roots, and its violet only shows while water moves through it: dry, it fades to buff. It grows in bright moss, so violet and moss green meet on every autumn forest floor. Here the night is amethyst and the action is moss: colour that exists only in circulation, like wealth in the manifesto.
